\chapter{Committee Portal Usefulness Survey}
\label{app:committee-survey}

This instrument is administered to University Scholarship Office staff and School Scholarship Subcommittee members after they complete Tasks 3 and 4 and the System Usability Scale. It addresses Research Question 2(b).

\section*{Respondent Profile}

Question 1: Which role did you perform during the testing session?

\begin{itemize}
  \item Question Type: Multiple Choice
  \item Options:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item University Scholarship Office staff
    \item School Scholarship Subcommittee member
    \item Other (Please specify)
  \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\section*{Perceived Usefulness}

Question 2: Please indicate how far you agree with each statement about the Data-Feeding Committee Portal.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Question Type: Likert Grid (5-point scale)
  \item Scale Columns: 5 --- Strongly Agree; 4 --- Agree; 3 --- Neutral; 2 --- Disagree; 1 --- Strongly Disagree
  \item Statements:
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item The portal presents the applicant information I need to make a decision.
    \item The school partition and filters reduce the manual collation of applicant files.
    \item The document checklist makes it easy to see which files are complete, missing, or illegible.
    \item The Conditional Revert workflow is clearer than rejecting an incomplete application outright.
    \item The audit log makes it clear who changed an applicant's status and when.
    \item I retain full control over every decision that changes an applicant's status.
    \item The screening flags inform my judgment without making the decision for me.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{itemize}
