\chapter{Post-Prototype Administrative Evaluation Survey}
\label{app:post-admin-survey}

Please answer the following based on your experience using the Unified Admin Dashboard of ScholarPath AdDU during the testing session. The same scales as in the pre-study survey are used so that your answers can be compared directly.

\section{Component 1: Perceived Processing Time}

\begin{enumerate}[series=q, label={Question \arabic*:}, leftmargin=*, align=left]
  \item Using ScholarPath AdDU, how much staff time do you estimate it would take to process a single scholarship application, from receiving it to marking it ready for interview or subcommittee evaluation?
  \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item Question Type: Multiple Choice
    \item Options:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
      \item Less than 15 minutes
      \item 15 -- 30 minutes
      \item 31 -- 60 minutes
      \item 1 -- 2 hours
      \item More than 2 hours
    \end{itemize}
  \end{itemize}
  \item Using ScholarPath AdDU, how long do you estimate one full application cycle would take, from the opening of applications to the release of results?
  \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item Question Type: Multiple Choice
    \item Options:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
      \item Less than 2 weeks
      \item 2 -- 4 weeks
      \item 1 -- 2 months
      \item 2 -- 3 months
      \item More than 3 months
    \end{itemize}
  \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\section{Component 2: Perceived Effort Scale}

\begin{enumerate}[resume=q, label={Question \arabic*:}, leftmargin=*, align=left]
  \item On a scale of 1 to 5, please rate how easy or difficult each of the following tasks would be for your office using ScholarPath AdDU.
  \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item Question Type: Likert Grid (5-point scale)
    \item Scale Columns:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
      \item 1 - Very Easy
      \item 2 - Easy
      \item 3 - Neutral
      \item 4 - Difficult
      \item 5 - Very Difficult
    \end{itemize}
    \item Row Sub-tasks:
    \begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*., leftmargin=*]
      \item Receiving and organizing incoming applications through the Unified Admin Dashboard.
      \item Checking baseline requirements using the flags produced by the Smart Eligibility Checker.
      \item Verifying uploaded documents in the Document Vault.
      \item Requesting corrected documents through the Conditional Revert window and tracking re-uploads.
      \item Viewing interview and subcommittee results in a single dashboard.
      \item Identifying who made a specific status change using the system's audit log.
      \item Informing applicants of status changes and deadlines through automated SMS and email notifications.
    \end{enumerate}
  \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}
